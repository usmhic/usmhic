# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone and to build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

Harassment, threats, derogatory comments, publishing private information, and
other unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove contributions or comments that violate this policy and
may temporarily or permanently ban harmful participants. Report sensitive
incidents privately through GitHub's repository reporting tools.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
